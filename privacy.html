Calendora privacy

Calendora is a shared family calendar. This page explains what it stores and why.

Signing in

When you sign in with Google, Calendora receives your name, email address, profile picture and Google's account ID. It uses them only to know who you are and which family you belong to. Calendora does not receive your Google password.

Your family's data

Events, chores, lists, meals, recipes, settings and photos you add are stored on Calendora's server (Cloudflare) so that every signed-in device in your family sees the same board. Only people and tablets signed in to your family can read it. Families cannot see each other's data.

Connected services (optional)

  • Google Calendar: if you connect it, your browser talks to Google directly to read and change the calendars you choose. A copy of those events is kept for your family so other family devices can show them.
  • Apple calendars: public calendar links you add are read by Calendora's server, and a copy is kept in case Apple can't be reached.
  • AnyList: if you connect it, your AnyList email and password are stored encrypted and used only to sync your shopping list.
  • Weather, news and the verse of the day are fetched from public services; your personal data is not sent to them, apart from the location you choose for the weather.

What Calendora doesn't do

No ads, no selling or sharing of your data, no tracking across other sites.

Removing your data

You can delete anything from inside Calendora, leave a family at any time in Settings → Family & sign-in, and the family's owner can remove people. To have a whole family deleted, ask the family's owner to contact the person who runs this Calendora.
